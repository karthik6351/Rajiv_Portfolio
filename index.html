About The Cardiologist

Four Decades of Dedication to Clinical Excellence & Patient Trust

Dr. Rajiv Karnik is a highly revered Senior Consultant Cardiologist and Interventionalist at Fortis Hospital, Mulund, Mumbai, whose career reflects a benchmark blend of academic brilliance, clinical innovation, and empathetic bedside care.

After topping Bombay University examinations across MBBS, MD (Medicine), and DM (Cardiology) with an extraordinary six gold medals, Dr. Karnik pursued high-level global cardiology training. He was selected for the prestigious Commonwealth Scholarship at Freeman Hospital, University of Newcastle-upon-Tyne (UK), and served as a Visiting Clinician at the Mayo Clinic in Rochester, USA.

Clinical Specialities

Interventional Cardiology & Cardiac Device Procedures

Providing comprehensive diagnostic catheterisation, complex coronary revascularisation, and cutting-edge electrical rhythm therapies.

Coronary Angioplasty & Stenting

Minimally invasive percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI) using drug-eluting stents to reopen blocked arteries, treat acute heart attacks, and alleviate angina.

  • Trans-Radial (wrist access) procedure
  • Drug-eluting stent (DES) implantation
  • Complex bifurcations & rotablation

Cardiac Catheterisation & Angiography

The gold standard diagnostic examination to visualize coronary blood vessels, measure intracardiac pressures, and determine the optimal course of medical or surgical therapy.

  • High-definition cine-angiography
  • Radial approach with same-day discharge
  • Fractional flow reserve (FFR) evaluation

Permanent Pacemaker Implantation

Implantation and routine interrogation of sophisticated single and dual-chamber pacemakers to treat bradycardia, AV block, and fainting disorders (syncope).

  • Single & Dual-Chamber pacing
  • Leadless pacemaker assessments
  • Wireless device programming & follow-up

Implantable Defibrillators (ICD)

Dr. Karnik pioneered the first ICD implant in Bombay. ICDs deliver life-saving therapy to prevent Sudden Cardiac Death (SCD) in high-risk heart disease patients.

  • 24/7 protection against ventricular fibrillation
  • Post-myocardial infarction risk mitigation
  • Subcutaneous and transvenous systems

Heart Failure & CRT Resynchronization

Tailored pharmacological management and Biventricular Pacing (CRT-P / CRT-D) to coordinate pumping action and improve ejection fraction in weakened hearts.

  • Guideline-directed medical therapy (GDMT)
  • Cardiac Resynchronization Therapy (CRT)
  • Echocardiographic monitoring & optimization

Preventive & Executive Cardiology

Proactive cardiovascular risk assessment, lipid fractionation, hypertension management, and lifestyle medicine to prevent first-time heart attacks.

  • ApoB, hs-CRP & advanced lipid profiling
  • Treadmill Stress Testing (TMT) interpretation
  • Individualized preventive wellness plans

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about cardiology consultations, heart procedures, and recovery.

Coronary Angiography is purely a diagnostic test where a tiny catheter and contrast dye are used to take high-resolution X-ray pictures of your coronary arteries to check for blockages. Angioplasty (PCI), on the other hand, is the treatment procedure performed if significant blockages are found; a tiny balloon is inflated to stretch open the blocked artery, and a permanent drug-eluting stent is placed to keep it open.
Performing angioplasty through the radial artery in the wrist significantly reduces the risk of bleeding complications compared to the traditional groin (femoral) approach. It also enables patients to sit up immediately after the procedure, walk comfortably within 1 to 2 hours, and usually be discharged the following morning.

During the first 4 weeks, avoid raising the arm on the implant side above shoulder level and refrain from lifting heavy weights. Modern devices are well shielded against common household appliances (such as microwave ovens and televisions), but you should keep your mobile phone in the pocket opposite the implant. Always carry your device ID card, and inform doctors before undergoing MRI scans or electrocautery.
